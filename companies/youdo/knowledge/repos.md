## Relationships

`gradle` builds the CI image -> `android-youdo4` builds APK/AAB -> `fastlane-docker` publishes release artifacts to stores, while test APKs go to Nexus -> `youdo-android-testing` runs through Selenium/Grid and Appium -> runner/farm configuration comes from `automation-services` -> device metrics are supplied by `android-exporter`.

For the planned dynamic Android application runner, `selectel-tf/prod-mks`
creates the dedicated `mks-infra` CI node group, `infra-tf/prod` owns the runner
namespace/Helm/RBAC/Vault resources, and `android-youdo4/.gitlab-ci.yml` selects
the Kubernetes runner and declares job resource/cache behavior. The detailed
map is in `~/ai/current/knowledge/systems/gitlab-ci/android-youdo4-dynamic-runner.md` (formerly `~/.codex/knowledge/gitlab-ci/android-youdo4-dynamic-runner.md`).

For B2B ephemeral development deployment, `gitlab-ci-templates` triggers `jenkins-pipelines`; Jenkins combines `helm-charts` with each service repository's `devops/dev.yml` and deploys to the Yandex dev cluster managed through `yandex-tf` and `infra-tf`. The authoritative map and staged service order are in `~/ai/current/knowledge/systems/dev-deployment/` (formerly `~/.codex/knowledge/dev-deployment.md`).
